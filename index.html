Muslim Now - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

At Muslim Now ("the app", "we"), we care about our users' privacy. This policy explains what data is collected while using the app and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Location Information: The app requests access to your device's GPS location in order to accurately calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This information is used solely for calculation purposes and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Prayer Tracking Data: Information about your prayers (completed/missed) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server and remains solely on your device.

How We Use Your Data

  • Location data: to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction
  • Prayer tracking data: stored only on your device for your personal use

No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising purposes.

Data Security

Data stored on your device (prayer tracking information) is accessible only to you. This data is removed from your device when you uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as new features are added to the app. Updates will be posted on this page.
